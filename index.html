How it works

1. Connect the wallet holding your Agent ID (the .ai name bound to your Agentic Wallet).
2. Check in once per UTC day. Check in on consecutive days to grow your streak; miss a day and it restarts at 1.
3. Your streak lives on-chain in the AgentStreak contract — no account, no sign-up, no server stores anything about you.

Checking in costs only network gas (a few cents at most). There is no token, no fee, and no reward: AgentStreak promises nothing except your streak. The contract is permissionless — it has no owner, no admin functions, and no way to pause or change the rules.
